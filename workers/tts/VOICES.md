# Reference voices for cloning (Chatterbox and Qwen)

Only voices whose speakers agreed in writing to voice cloning and commercial
use may be cloned. The worker enforces this: `linguabc_tts/voices.py` loads a
voice only if its record has a signed consent covering `voice-cloning` and
`commercial-use`, a licence, a person's approval, an unchanged audio hash, and
no expiry or revocation.

## What to record (per voice)

- **Who:** a professional voice actor or a staff member, adult, native speaker
  of the accent: British (one female, one male), Australian, American.
- **Consent:** a signed agreement that names LinguABC, allows creating a
  synthetic voice from the recording, commercial use in learning materials, and
  says how consent can be withdrawn. Keep the signed file outside git; the
  record points to it (`consent.document`).
- **Recording:** 10-15 s of natural speech, one take, quiet room (no echo, no
  music), close microphone, WAV 44.1 or 48 kHz, no processing. The **first
  10 seconds matter most**: Chatterbox conditions on the first 10 s (decoder)
  and the first 6 s (prompt); Qwen uses the whole clip plus its transcript.
- **Script:** neutral and typical, e.g. *"Good morning. I'd like to tell you a
  little about our city library. It opens at half past nine, and you can borrow
  up to six books at a time."* Speak at an IELTS pace (about 150 words a minute).
- **Transcript:** the exact words, punctuation included.

Not accepted: clips from films, YouTube or podcasts; datasets whose consent
covers research only; Common Voice clips (identifiable volunteers; used only
for a technical accent test, never for production); voices generated by
another commercial TTS.

## Steps

1. Write the record (`consent-template.json` in this folder shows every field).
2. `python -m linguabc_tts.voices_cli add --registry <voices> --audio raw.wav --record rec.json`
   checks consent and licence, then the recording (length, clipping, noise,
   long pauses), trims it, resamples to 24 kHz mono, normalises to -20 LUFS and
   stores it **unapproved**. Problems say what to re-record.
3. Listen to the prepared WAV, check the signed consent, then
   `voices_cli approve --id <id> --by <name>`.
4. Test the voice with the bench (`pilot/bench/run_bench.py`) and the accent
   suite; add it to the blind review against Fish.
5. Deploy: the registry folder is mounted read-only into the worker
   (`/voices`). The worker loads only usable voices.
6. If the speaker withdraws consent: `voices_cli revoke --id <id>`. The worker
   refuses the voice from its next start.
